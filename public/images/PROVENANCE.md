Each prompt used the following opening and closing with the garment and view filled in.

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: Attraqtive Label sample product gallery, [view] view of the [garment]. Use the supplied on-model image as the garment reference. Preserve the exact visible colour, fabric, silhouette, trim, pattern and construction of this garment.

- Product View: “Full length front catalogue photo of the main garment and included draped layers on a headless, armless neutral tailoring form, no human, no jewellery or shoes. Centre it with the whole garment visible, with modest margins. Warm light grey seamless studio background.”
- Details: “Tight close-up catalogue photo of the garment fabric and its most recognizable construction detail, using only details visible in the reference. Fabric fills the frame. No human, hands, skin, jewellery, hangers or props.”

> Photorealistic, soft neutral light, portrait composition, no text, logos, collage or watermark.
