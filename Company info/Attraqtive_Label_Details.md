## 8. Brand positioning inferred for website planning

**Suggested category label:** *Designer Couture & Bespoke Occasionwear Boutique*.

**Publicly supported strengths:**
1. Bespoke bridal and wedding designs.
2. Indian craftsmanship combined with contemporary styling.
3. Broad occasionwear coverage across women, men, and children.
4. Personalized consultations, customization, and fittings.
5. Strong customer-review sentiment around fit, finish, and responsiveness.

**Suggested audience segments (inference, not owner-confirmed):** Brides, wedding parties, families seeking coordinated outfits, clients looking for custom formalwear, and parents seeking children's occasionwear.

**Suggested website positioning sentence (original proposed copy, not an official brand statement):**
> Thoughtfully crafted couture for life's most memorable occasions.

## 9. Recommended demo website information architecture

1. **Home** — brand hero, featured couture imagery, design philosophy, consultation CTA.
2. **Collections** — Bridal Couture, Designer Gowns, Occasionwear, Men's Designer Wear, Kids' Designer Wear; confirm which categories the owner wants featured.
3. **Bespoke Experience** — consultation, design inspiration, fabric selection, fitting, and delivery; validate process with owner.
4. **Our Story** — founder/brand narrative, establishment year, design philosophy; avoid inventing biography.
5. **Client Stories** — permission-cleared testimonials and photos.
6. **Visit / Contact** — map, confirmed business hours, phone/WhatsApp, appointment request.

**Recommended demo CTAs:** “Explore Collections,” “Enquire About a Design,” “Book a Consultation” (only if appointments are actually offered).

**Suggested visual direction (proposal, not observed brand standards):** Editorial luxury fashion photography, elegant serif typography, ivory/charcoal/champagne palette, generous whitespace, mobile-first layout. Final colors should follow their actual logo and preferences.
