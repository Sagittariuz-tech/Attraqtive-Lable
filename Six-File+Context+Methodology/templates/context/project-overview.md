# Attraqtive Label

## Overview

Sagittariuz is building a digital flagship for Attraqtive Label, a premium designer boutique in Ram Nagar, Visakhapatnam. The first deliverable is a private interactive demonstration of editorial discovery, garment detail, retail checkout and bespoke consultation. Use the prospect-supplied phone +91 90599 98264. Owner approval is pending for the logo, story, live catalogue, policies, full address, hours, social accounts and WhatsApp channel.

## Goals

1. Present couture through fashion photography, editorial typography and mobile-first navigation.
2. Let visitors browse, compare model and product views, save garments and complete a simulated checkout.
3. Give bespoke visitors a consultation route and show the proposed image discovery experiences.

## Core User Flow

1. Arrive through the homepage, collection or search.
2. Filter the catalogue and open a product or quick view.
3. Compare gallery views and choose an eligible size and colour.
4. Save the product, add a variant to the bag or take the consultation route.
5. Review quantities and subtotal, then complete a simulated guest checkout.
6. For bespoke enquiries, complete a local form or preview a stylist message.

## Features

### Demo collections
- Proposed bridal couture, occasionwear and contemporary ethnic are the initial editorial categories. Bespoke is a parallel service. Lehengas, gowns, women's designerwear, men's and children's occasionwear are configurable proposals.
- Display clearly labeled sample styles, prices, inventory and generated illustrative photography. Actual inventory and prices await owner data.
- The Atelier, bespoke journey, lookbook, boutique contact, account preview and policy approval placeholders support the owner presentation.

### Stage B commerce
- Approved catalogue, inventory, account and guest journeys, order and fulfillment operations, payments, customer support, content and consultation administration.

### Stage C discovery
- Virtual Atelier and Find Your Inspiration previews use presets and sample results. Real photo upload and processing require approval.

## Scope

Stage A uses local sample catalogue and browser state. Checkout and consultations provide on-screen confirmations. Stage B and C integrations require commercial approval.

## Success Criteria

A visitor can complete the retail and bespoke journeys with keyboard or touch, distinguish all sample data and concept features, and revisit saved items and bag lines after refresh. Review desktop, tablet and phone layouts, reduced motion and accessibility. See the progress tracker for actual test results.
